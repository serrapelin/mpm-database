Monetary Policy Meeting Database

MPM provides a standardized, machine-readable dataset of central bank policy meetings, policy-rate decisions, meeting calendars, and cross-country monetary policy synchronization. The database is built for research on international monetary transmission, central bank reaction functions, and the global spillovers of Federal Reserve policy.

What the database is for

Meeting-level evidence on monetary policy decisions

Monthly datasets can miss the timing of central bank decisions, especially when multiple policy meetings occur in the same month or when a local meeting follows a Federal Reserve announcement by only a few days. MPM is designed to preserve that timing.

Meeting frequency

Policy calendars

Each observation is organized around a central bank meeting date rather than a monthly aggregate, allowing high-frequency alignment across countries.

Policy decisions

Rate changes and decisions

The template includes fields for policy-rate levels, changes, decision signs, meeting windows, and links to source documents.

Global transmission

Fed synchronization

The structure supports studying whether central banks adjust policy in the same direction as recent Federal Reserve moves and high-frequency U.S. monetary policy shocks.

Research questions

What can be studied with MPM?

Do central banks follow the Fed?

Measure local monetary policy responses after FOMC meetings at the meeting frequency rather than relying only on monthly policy-rate changes.

Does aggregation hide policy synchronization?

Compare meeting-level decisions with monthly aggregates to quantify how timing and offsetting moves shape estimated spillovers.

How do advanced and emerging economies differ?

Study heterogeneity by market classification, inflation-targeting status, exchange-rate regime, or regional grouping.

How do policy decisions interact with communication?

Link decisions to statements, minutes, press releases, or macro announcements when source documents are available.
