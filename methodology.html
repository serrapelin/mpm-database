How MPM is built

A reproducible pipeline for building a cross-country, meeting-frequency central bank decision dataset.

Database features

Scope

Central bank monetary policy meetings, decision dates, policy-rate levels, rate changes, decision signs, and source links. Replace this text with the finalized scope of the first release.

Meeting alignment

Reproducibility

Versioned releases should include raw-source references, cleaned meeting-level files, a codebook, and scripts for validation checks.

Suggested construction pipeline

Collect official calendars and source documents

Start from central bank calendars, policy statements, press releases, and official policy-rate histories.

Standardize meeting dates and country identifiers

Use ISO country codes, central bank identifiers, local meeting dates, and consistent publication timestamps.

Construct decision variables

Record the policy-rate level, basis-point change, direction of move, unscheduled meetings, and missing-data flags.

Merge global event timing

Add FOMC dates, recent U.S. monetary policy shocks, and windows since the last Fed meeting for cross-country synchronization analysis.

Validate and version releases

Run duplicate checks, date-order checks, rate-change consistency checks, and country-level source audits before each release.

Future updates

More jurisdictions

Add countries as source coverage and validation improve.

Text links

Connect each meeting to statements, minutes, and press conference transcripts when available.
